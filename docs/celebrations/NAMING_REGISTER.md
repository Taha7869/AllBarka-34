# Celebrations naming register

12 November 2026 (Asia/Karachi). Documentation proposal only; no runtime/catalog mutation.

Owner approved the previous recommended names subject to the corrections here. Changed signatures, plain-only ranges, corrected slugs and the production plan await the requested final yes. Unchanged signatures retain their approval. Every Arabic string (signature AND plain name, including standard terms) is **needs native review** until an identified native reviewer signs off. Review fields live in the coverage tracker; no native approval is claimed.

Signature register: Roman Urdu for Wedding, Baby, Birthdays, Hajj, Umrah, Religious, Family, School and Accessories; English for Corporate. Birthdays/School register selections complete the one-register rule and await final yes. Zero selected signature names use “chapter”; keep at most one in future names. ## Publication and commerce decisions

An occasion without a finished, explicitly publication-approved design appears in the hub as “Request this occasion”. Its canonical URL renders the existing enquiry experience with noindex and is excluded from the sitemap. No empty grid. Redirect aliases lead to that same noindex page until eligible. A generated image alone does not satisfy finished-design/publication readiness. Finished means named artwork, feasible formats, checked media or deliberate zero-image presentation, localized content, functional selection/enquiry and owner-approved display status. All unmanufactured designs retain a visible data-driven “Design concept” caption even when indexable. Arabic review status remains explicit; do not claim native sign-off.

Prices, shipping, coupons, checkout, auth and server pricing logic stay untouched. Approved real product prices use the existing commerce contract; unpriced design selections use the existing enquiry flow.
