# Care copy basis

Product-specific serving suggestions are culinary editorial copy, not health claims. Regions follow the existing catalog or the named product; unidentified sources are stated as unconfirmed. No new certification or farm provenance is asserted.

Storage windows are conservative quality-rotation guidance under the stated conditions, not a tested AllBarka shelf life. The earlier printed batch date takes priority. Oil and cosmetic-use grades must be checked on the actual bottle before use.

- [University of Georgia / USDA Food Keeper guide](https://extension.uga.edu/content/dam/extension-county-offices/gwinnett-county/facs/Food_Keeper_Guide.pdf): opened nuts, dry-fruit quality and refrigeration guidance.
- [National Center for Home Food Preservation: dried foods](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/dry/general-information-drying/packaging-and-storing-dried-foods/): dry, cool, airtight storage and temperature-dependent quality.
- [Utah State University: food packaging](https://extension.usu.edu/preserve-the-harvest/food-storage-packaging): herbs/spices protected from air, light and moisture.
- [FDA: food allergies](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/food-allergies): tree-nut identity, sesame and ingredient checks; sulfite status is not assumed without batch evidence.
- [CDC: honey and infants](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/foods-and-drinks/foods-and-drinks-to-avoid-or-limit.html): honey must not be served to children under 12 months.

Owner review still needs actual lot dates, spice-blend ingredient declarations and food/cosmetic grades. Those facts are not invented in this copy.
